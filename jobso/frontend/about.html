About JOBSO

JOBSO is a lightweight job board designed to help candidates discover roles and employers find great talent quickly. We built this prototype to demonstrate a simple, secure flow for posting jobs, applying with a resume, and managing applicants.

Our mission

Make hiring more efficient by connecting qualified candidates with companies through a clean, fast interface.
